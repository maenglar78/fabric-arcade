FEATURE

Iterative Learning

Four tiers of games, calibrated by complexity and time. Start with a 5-minute Arcade, end with a full Mission. Grow at your own pace.

🎚️ The four tiers

~5–15 min · ⭐

Arcade

Bite-sized, single-concept games. Goal: get one Fabric primitive (a KQL table, a measure, a pipeline step) to work end-to-end. Perfect for a coffee break or a workshop warm-up.

~15–30 min · ⭐⭐

Challenge

Mini-projects with two or three moving parts. You connect a stream to a destination, or you build a small star schema. Comes with a scored validation notebook.

~30–60 min · ⭐⭐⭐

Puzzle / Quest

Open-ended design puzzles with multiple valid solutions and an elegance score. Calc Groups Cathedral lives here: 12 KPIs, find the most elegant DAX to express them all.

~45–120 min · ⭐⭐⭐

Mission

Full end-to-end scenarios. Multiple workloads, multiple notebooks, a story to follow. Ontology Detective and Monster Breach are missions.

📈 Suggested path

Most learners go from one tier to the next by topic, not all at once. A typical path for Real-Time Intelligence:

  • 🎯 Arcade: Target Practice (Eventstream → Eventhouse, 15 min)
  • 🏎️ Mission: Fabric Racing Game (live telemetry, JSON mapping, KQL dashboard, 30 min)
  • ️ Mission: Ontology Detective (Digital Twin + KQL investigation, 2 hours)

For Power BI / DAX: jump straight to Calc Groups Cathedral — it is the most concentrated DAX lesson in the catalog.

🔁 Why "iterative"

Every game ends with a scored validation notebook. That score is not a grade — it is a hint. "You passed, but your DAX has three nested CALCULATE calls. Can you do it in one?" Re-run, refactor, learn. The check notebook accepts unlimited attempts and your best run wins the badge.
