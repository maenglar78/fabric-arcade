🎯 Why "real" matters

You can learn KQL from slides. You can learn DAX from blog posts. But until you have actually:

  • Hit a Direct Lake fallback because a calc group was wrong
  • Forgotten to publish an Eventstream and wondered why no data flows
  • Watched a Custom Endpoint dropping events because the JSON mapping was off by one field

…you do not really know the platform. Fabric Arcade makes those mistakes cheap and fun.

💰 Capacity footprint

All games are designed to run on an F2 capacity. Eventhouses and KQL DBs are the most expensive piece, so you can pause or delete them when you are done. The arcade.uninstall() command will clean up everything the game created.
