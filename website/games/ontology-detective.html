"The rain hasn't stopped in Datapolis for three days. My coffee's cold, the phone won't shut up, and another case just landed on my desk. The kind of case you don't solve with a hunch — you solve it with a knowledge graph."

📋 Overview

You are Detective Sherlock Graph at Datapolis P.I., the city's last honest investigation agency. Five cases. Five mysteries. Each one is solved by designing the right ontology in the Fabric Digital Twin Builder UI, then writing a KQL query against the case evidence that converges on a single suspect.

The ontology is your thinking tool. The KQL is your weapon. The judge — detective.accuse(case_id, name) — doesn't care how pretty your model is, only that you can name the culprit.

from fabric_arcade import arcade

# 2. Run OntologyDetective_Seed (one-time, loads all 5 case datasets)
# 3. Open OntologyDetective_CaseFile and play

🧩 How You Play (Real Fabric, Not a Simulation)

Each case follows the same investigative loop:

  1. 📖 Read the noir briefing in the OntologyDetective_CaseFile notebook — victim, time window, evidence tables, suspect list.
  2. 🏛️ Open DetectiveOntology in the Fabric Digital Twin Builder UI and add the entity types & relationships the briefing calls for (e.g. Person, Room, Visit; or Person, Alias, sameAs).
  3. 🔍 Write a KQL query against the case evidence tables in Datapolis_DetectiveEH until it returns exactly one suspect.
  4. ⚖️ Call detective.accuse("case-id", "Suspect Name") — the judge confirms or releases them, and logs the event to the Eventhouse.
  5. 📊 The OntologyDetective_Dashboard shows cases solved, wrong calls, and your detective rank.
  6. 🏅 Solve all 5 cases, set your PLAYER_NAME, and run the badge cell to mint your Commissioner of Datapolis badge — shareable on LinkedIn.

📦 What Gets Installed in Your Workspace

arcade.install("ontology-detective") deploys:

  • ⚡ 1 Eventhouse Datapolis_DetectiveEH — holds the DetectiveEvents telemetry table plus the 8 evidence tables (one per case data source).
  • 🌐 1 Ontology DetectiveOntology — pre-created empty. You design the entities and relationships in Digital Twin Builder as each case demands.
  • 📓 3 Notebooks:
    • OntologyDetective_Seed — applies the KQL schema and bulk-loads all 5 case datasets (run once).
    • OntologyDetective_CaseFile — 5 noir briefings + detective object (briefing, accuse, scoreboard) + badge cell.
    • OntologyDetective_Dashboard — leaderboard, recent events, accuracy.

🔍 Example: Case 1 KQL

The briefing tells you Mrs. Plum's pie went missing from the kitchen between 14:00 and 14:30. Three neighbours visited. Find the one who was in the kitchen during the window.

Case1_Visits
| where RoomName == "Kitchen"
| where EnteredAt < datetime(2026-06-05 14:30:00)
  and LeftAt   > datetime(2026-06-05 14:00:00)
| where PersonName != "Mrs. Plum"
| project PersonName

One row comes back. Pass that name to detective.accuse("stolen-pie", "...") and you're a Rookie.
